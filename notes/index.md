# Ujaval's Notes and Bookmarks

This site is a collection of my personal notes and bookmarks collected over the years. It is organized to help search and recall specific items or find notes related to a certain topic. 

If you'd like to setup a similar website for your own notes, see the detailed guide at [Building a Personal Knowledge Website](https://courses.spatialthoughts.com/agentic-coding-geospatial.html#building-a-personal-knowledge-website) section of our Agentic Coding for Geospatial course.

## Latest Finds

- [Inherited Reference Artefacts in Google's GEM-15](https://eartharxiv.org/repository/view/16205/): Independent dryland evaluation of Google's 15 m GEM-15 DEM against ICESat-2 and lidar, with a destriping fix for inherited AW3D30 artefacts. [Keywords: `GEM-15` `DEM` `ICESat-2` `destriping`] — [More in Data](Data.md)
- [Farewell to Human Readable Code](https://csailmit.substack.com/p/farewell-to-human-readable-code): Nir Shavit's essay arguing AI-optimized code will stop being human-readable and must be tested by behavior instead. [Keywords: `AI coding` `code readability` `software engineering`] — [More in AI](AI.md)
- [Timing the Flames: Geostationary Detection of Shifting Stubble Burning](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/18/10/1506): Paper showing NW India stubble burning shifted to late afternoon after 2022, evading MODIS/VIIRS overpasses but caught by geostationary GEO-KOMPSAT-2A. [Keywords: `stubble burning` `fire detection` `geostationary satellite` `air quality`] — [More in Remote Sensing](Remote_Sensing.md)
